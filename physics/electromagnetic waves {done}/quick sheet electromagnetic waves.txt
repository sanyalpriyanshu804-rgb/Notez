1. DISPLACEMENT CURRENT AND RC CIRCUIT

Capacitor Charging Equations:
- Plate Charge: q(t) = C V (1 - e⁻ᵗ/ᴿ⁻ᶜ)
- Conduction Current in Wires: I_c = dq/dt = (V/R) e⁻ᵗ/ᴿ⁻ᶜ
  📌 t = 0: I_c = V/R (short circuit); t → ∞: I_c = 0 (open circuit).
- Electric Field in Gap: E(t) = q(t)/(A ε₀) = [C V (1 - e⁻ᵗ/ᴿ⁻ᶜ)] / (A ε₀)
- Electric Flux (radius r ≤ R_plate): Φ_E = E π r² = [C V π r² / (A ε₀)] (1 - e⁻ᵗ/ᴿ⁻ᶜ)
- Displacement Current Definition: ⭐ I_d = ε₀ (dΦ_E/dt)
- Displacement Current (radius r): I_d(r) = (V/R) (π r² / A) e⁻ᵗ/ᴿ⁻ᶜ
- Total Gap Displacement Current (r = R_plate): ⭐ I_d = (V/R) e⁻ᵗ/ᴿ⁻ᶜ = I_c
- Total Current Continuity: ⭐ I_total = I_c + I_d = (V/R) e⁻ᵗ/ᴿ⁻ᶜ everywhere at all t.

Internal Magnetic Field (r ≤ R_plate):
- Ampere-Maxwell Law: ∫ B · dl = μ₀ I_d(r) ⇒ B (2 π r) = μ₀ I_d(r)
- Magnetic Field Formula: ⭐ B(r, t) = (μ₀ I_c r)/(2 A) = (μ₀ I_c r)/(2 π R_plate²) = [μ₀ V r / (2 R A)] e⁻ᵗ/ᴿ⁻ᶜ
  Units: Tesla (T).
- Spatial Trend (fixed t): B ∝ r (linear, B = 0 at r = 0, max at r = R_plate).
- Temporal Trend (fixed r): B ∝ e⁻ᵗ/ᴿ⁻ᶜ (exponential decay to 0 at steady state).
- Maximum Internal Field (r = R_plate): ⭐ B_max(t) = (μ₀ I_c)/(2 π R_plate)

2. MAXWELL'S FOUR EQUATIONS

1. Gauss's Law for Electrostatics: ⭐ ∫ E · dA = q_in / ε₀
   Meaning: Electric charges act as sources/sinks of E field lines.
2. Gauss's Law for Magnetism: ⭐ ∫ B · dA = 0
   Meaning: Magnetic monopoles do not exist; B field lines form closed loops.
3. Faraday's Law of Induction: ⭐ ∫ E · dl = -dΦ_B/dt
   Meaning: Time-varying magnetic flux induces non-conservative E field loops.
4. Ampere-Maxwell Law: ⭐ ∫ B · dl = μ₀ (I_c + I_d) = μ₀ I_c + μ₀ ε₀ (dΦ_E/dt)
   Meaning: Magnetic fields arise from moving charges (I_c) and changing E fields (I_d).

3. CURRENT TYPES COMPARISON

- Physical Origin: I_c = drift of electrons; I_d = time-varying electric flux (dΦ_E/dt).
- Location: I_c in wires/resistors; I_d in capacitor gap.
- Formulas: I_c = dq/dt; I_d = ε₀ (dΦ_E/dt).
- Gap Magnitudes: I_c = 0; I_d = (V/R) e⁻ᵗ/ᴿ⁻ᶜ.
- Wire Magnitudes: I_c = (V/R) e⁻ᵗ/ᴿ⁻ᶜ; I_d = 0.
- Magnetic Field Generation: Both I_c and I_d generate magnetic fields.

4. WAVE MECHANICS & TRANSVERSE TRAVELING WAVE

Wave Equation:
y(x, t) = A sin(k x ± ω t + ϕ)  or  A cos(k x ± ω t + ϕ)
- A = peak amplitude.
- k = angular wave number = 2π/λ (rad/m), Dimensions: [L⁻¹].
- ω = angular frequency = 2π/T = 2π ν (rad/s), Dimensions: [T⁻¹].
- Wave Speed: ⭐ v = ν λ = ω/k
💡 Shortcut: ω = k v ("ω = KV rule").

Direction Rules:
- Position variable in bracket (x, y, z) = axis of propagation.
- Opposite signs (k x - ω t) or (-k x + ω t) → POSITIVE direction (+x).
- Same signs (k x + ω t) or (-k x - ω t) → NEGATIVE direction (-x).

5. PROPERTIES OF ELECTROMAGNETIC WAVES

Sources:
- Charge at rest (v = 0, a = 0): Static E field.
- Charge at uniform v (a = 0): Constant E and B fields; no wave radiation.
- Accelerated/oscillating charge (a ≠ 0): Continuously radiates EM waves.

Properties:
- Non-Mechanical: Propagates through vacuum and media without material support.
- Light Vector: E field vector is the optical vector (retinal/optical forces driven by q E, since v << c ⇒ q(v×B) << qE).
- Transverse Vector Triad: E ⊥ B ⊥ v.
  ⭐ E · B = 0, E · v = 0, B · v = 0
- Propagation Direction: ⭐ v_cap = e_cap × b_cap
- Polarization Direction: ⭐ Polarization direction = e_cap (orientation of E field).
- Phase Synchronicity: E and B oscillate in EXACT SAME PHASE (Δϕ = 0).
  E(x, t) = E₀ j_cap sin(k x - ω t), B(x, t) = B₀ k_cap sin(k x - ω t)
- Field Amplitude Ratios:
  ⭐ Vacuum: E₀ / B₀ = c ⇒ E₀ = B₀ c ; E_rms / B_rms = c
  ⭐ Medium: E₀ / B₀ = v ⇒ E₀ = B₀ v
- Medium Boundaries:
  📌 Frequency f (ν) remains strictly CONSTANT (source dependent).
  Speed v and wavelength λ change: v = f λ.
  Vacuum speed: c = 1/√(μ₀ ε₀) ≈ 3 × 10⁸ m/s.
  Medium speed: v = 1/√(μ ε) = 1/√(μ₀ μ_r ε₀ ε_r).
  Refractive Index: n = c/v = √(μ_r ε_r).
  For non-magnetic media (μ_r = 1): ⭐ n = √ε_r = √K  (K = dielectric constant).

6. ENERGY DENSITIES AND INTENSITY

Averages & RMS:
- <sin²> = 1/2, <cos²> = 1/2
- E_rms = E₀ / √2, B_rms = B₀ / √2

Energy Densities:
- Instantaneous: u_E = (1/2) ε₀ E², u_B = B² / (2 μ₀)
- Average Electric Energy Density: ⭐ <u_E> = (1/4) ε₀ E₀² = (1/2) ε₀ E_rms²
- Average Magnetic Energy Density: ⭐ <u_B> = B₀² / (4 μ₀) = B_rms² / (2 μ₀)
- Equal Energy Partition: ⭐ <u_E> = <u_B>
- Total Average Energy Density:
  ⭐ <u_total> = <u_E> + <u_B> = 2 <u_E> = (1/2) ε₀ E₀² = ε₀ E_rms² = B₀² / (2 μ₀) = B_rms² / μ₀
  Units: J/m³, Dimensions: [M L⁻¹ T⁻²].

Intensity (I):
- Definition: Power / Area = Energy / (Area × Time) (W/m², [M T⁻³]).
- Relation to Energy Density: ⭐ I = <u_total> c
- Intensity Formulas:
  ⭐ I = (1/2) ε₀ E₀² c = ε₀ E_rms² c = (B₀² c) / (2 μ₀) = (B_rms² c) / μ₀
- Peak Field from Intensity: ⭐ E₀ = √[(2 I) / (ε₀ c)]
- Point Source (Isotropic): ⭐ I(r) = P / (4 π r²) ∝ 1/r²
  Field Amplitudes: E₀(r) ∝ 1/r, B₀(r) ∝ 1/r.
