MAGNETISM AND MATTER REVISION SHEET

Properties of magnet -
a) Magnet attract material like iron, nickel, cobalt etc.
b) Isolated magnetic poles or monopoles do not exist.
c) Like poles repel each other while unlike poles attract.
d) Magnets have directive properties, they align themselves in North-South direction when suspended freely.
e) Magnet induces magnetism in magnetic material when placed near it. This is called magnetic induction

Properties of magnetic field lines -
a) Magnetic field lines travels from North to South outside the magnet and opposite inside.
b) Magnetic field lines form closed loops.
c) Two magnetic field lines never intersect each other because if they do, two directions.
d) Magnetic field lines exist inside every magnetized material.

1. LOOPS AND DIPOLES
* Formula: M = NIA (N: turns, I: current, A: area). Unit: A.m²
* Circle has max Area (and M) for fixed perimeter.
* Direction: Right-hand thumb rule. Anti-clockwise face = North.
* Folded loops: M_net = √(M₁² + M₂² + 2M₁M₂cosθ). 90 deg fold = M√2.

Magnetic dipole : A magnetic dipole consist of two unlike poles of equal strength seperate by small distance.
Magnetic dipole moment(M) : It is the product of pole strength of either pole and magnetic length of magnet. Direction South to North. Unit Ampere-meter².

2. FIELDS AND FORCES
* Torque τ = M x B = MB sin θ. Max at 90 deg, zero at 0/180 deg.
* Energy U = -M⋅B = -MB cos θ.
* Work W = MB(cos θ₁ - cos θ₂).
* Stable: 0 deg (U = -MB); Unstable: 180 deg (U = +MB).
* SHM Time Period: T = 2π √(I/MB). Small θ required.

3. BAR MAGNETS
* Moment M = m⋅L (Pole strength x length). Direction: South to North.
* Pole strength m ∝ Area. Unit: A-m.
* No monopoles: Gauss Law ∫B.dA = 0.    # net magnetic flux through a closed surface is always zero, magnetic monopoles do not exist.
* Cutting: M' = M/n (Transverse or Longitudinal).
* Bending: Semicircle M' = 2M/π; L-shape M' = M/√2; Circle M' = 0.
* Electrostatics Analogy: p ⇄ M, E ⇄ B, k ⇄ μ₀/4π. Axial B = (μ₀/4π)2M/r³.

Magnetizing field intensity or magnetising force(H) : When a substances of soft iron core is kept inside solenoid then it also get some magnetised. The property of a field to magnetize substance is called magnetizing field intensity or magnetising force.

Diamagnetic substance : Diamagnetic substances are those in which the individual atoms molecules ions don't possess any net magnetic moment on their own.
    When such substances are kept in external magnetic field they get weekly magnetized in opposite direction to magnetising field.
    Electrons are found in pair and in opposite direction thus they cancel each others magnetic effect.

4. EARTH'S MAGNETISM
* Horizontal Component: B_h = B_e cos δ.
* Vertical Component: B_v = B_e sin δ.
* Tangent Law: tan δ = B_v/B_h.
* Equator: δ = 0, B_v = 0, B_h = B_e.
* Poles: δ = 90, B_h = 0, B_v = B_e.
* Apparent Dip: tan δ' = tan δ_true / cos β.

5. ATOMIC AND MATERIALS
* Electron Orbit: M = evr/2 = eL/2m. M ∝ n (orbit number).
* Gyromagnetic Ratio: M/L = e/2m = 8.8 x 10¹⁰ C/kg (Constant for an electron).
* Susceptibility χ = I/H. Relation: μ_r = 1 + χ.

* Diamagnets: χ small/negative, temp independent.
* Paramagnets: χ small/positive, χ ∝ 1/T (Curie Law).
* Ferromagnets: χ large/positive. Above Tc, becomes Para.

* Soft Iron: Low Coercivity, High Permeability (Electromagnets).
* Steel: High Coercivity (Permanent magnets).
