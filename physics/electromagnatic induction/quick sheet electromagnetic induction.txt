1. FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTROMAGNETIC INDUCTION (EMI)
- Definition: Generation of EMF and current in a conductor by changing magnetic environment or flux link.
- Physical Meaning: Manipulation of magnetic fields to obtain electricity.
- Core Observation: Current is only observed during a change in B, Area, or Orientation.
- Nature loves symmetry: Current creates B-field, so changing B-field creates current.

2. MAGNETIC FLUX (Φᵦ)
- Definition: Scalar quantity representing the total number of field lines piercing a surface.
- Formula (Uniform): Φᵦ = B ⋅ A = BA cos θ.
- Formula (General): Φᵦ = ∫B dA (Use for non-uniform fields).
- Variables: B = Magnetic field; A = Area vector; θ = Angle between B and NORMAL to the plane.
- Units: Weber (Wb) or Tesla-meter² (T.m²).
- Dimensions: [ML²T⁻²A⁻¹].
- 📌 Condition: θ is ALWAYS measured from the normal to the surface, not the surface itself.
- 💡 Shortcut: Total magnetic flux through any closed surface is zero (no monopoles).
- 💡 Shared Flux Principle: If field exists only in an inner region, flux through any larger outer loop is identical to flux through inner region.

3. FARADAY'S AND LENZ'S LAWS
- Faraday's Law: Induced EMF magnitude = rate of change of magnetic flux.
- ⭐ Formula: e = -dΦ/dt (For N turns: e = -N dΦ/dt).
- 📌 Condition: EMF exists even in insulators/vacuum; current requires a closed path.
- Lenz's Law: Direction of induced current opposes the change in flux that produced it.
- Intuition Rule: If flux ↑, loop opposes; if flux ↓, loop supports.
- Conservation of Energy: Mechanical work done against magnetic opposition converts to electrical energy.
- ⭐ Induced Current: i = e / R = (1/R) (dΦ/dt).
- ⭐ Induced Charge: q = ΔΦ / R.
- 📌 Condition: Induced charge (q) is independent of time.
- ⭐ Power Dissipated: P = e² / R.

4. MOTIONAL EMF
- Translational EMF: A moving rod acts as a battery due to Lorentz force on free electrons.
- ⭐ Formula: e = BvL.    # iska derivation rail wale se
- 📌 Condition: v, B, and L must be mutually perpendicular.
- Vector Form: e = (v x B) ⋅ L.
- 📌 Sign Convention: (v x B) direction indicates the high-potential (positive) terminal.
- 💡 Effective Length (Leff): In uniform B, shape is irrelevant; use displacement vector between endpoints.
- Rotational EMF: Rod of length L rotating about one end at angular speed ω.
- ⭐ Formula: e = 1/2 BωL².    # e = BLv ko integrate karke
- General Rotational Point: PD between points x₁ and x₂ = 1/2 Bω(x₂² - x₁²).
- AC Generator (Nawab Formula): e = NABω sin(ωt).
- Max EMF: e_max = NABω (when coil plane is parallel to B).

5. DYNAMICS OF RODS ON RAILS
- Retarding Force: F = B²L²v / R (opposes motion).
- Acceleration: a = -B²L²v / mR.
- ⭐ Velocity Decay: v = v₀ exp(-(B²L²/mR)t).
- ⭐ Terminal Velocity: v_terminal = mgR / (B²L²) (for rod falling under gravity).
- 📌 Condition: mg = IlB at terminal velocity.

6. INDUCTANCE
- General Definition: Property to oppose changes in current.
- Self Inductance (L): Φ_total = LI.
- ⭐ Self EMF (Back EMF): e = -L(dI/dt).
- ⭐ L of Solenoid: L = μ₀n²Al = μ₀N²A / l.
- 📌 Condition: If wound on iron core, L increases by factor μᵣ.
Unit : Henry
- Mutual Inductance (M): Φ₂ = MI₁.
- Reciprocity: M₁₂ = M₂₁.
- ⭐ Mutual EMF: e₂ = -M(dI₁/dt).
- ⭐ Energy Stored: U = 1/2 LI² (stored in magnetic field).    # iska derivation h
- Series Combination: L_eq = L₁ + L₂ ± 2M (+ for same flux sense, - for opposite).
- Parallel Combination (no M): 1/L_eq = 1/L₁ + 1/L₂.

7. RL CIRCUITS (TRANSIENT ANALYSIS)
- Growth of Current: i = i₀(1 - e⁻ᵗ/ᶿ).
- Decay of Current: i = i₀ e⁻ᵗ/ᶿ.
- ⭐ Time Constant: ᶿ = L / R.
- Steady State Current: i₀ = E / R.
- 📌 Condition: t = 0, inductor = open circuit; t = ∞, inductor = short circuit.
- 💡 Shortcut: Current reaches 63% max in 1ᶿ during growth; drops to 37% in 1ᶿ during decay.
- 💡 Shortcut: Time to reach half-max current: t = ᶿ ln(2).

8. INDUCED ELECTRIC FIELDS
- Origin: Produced by time-varying magnetic fields (dB/dt).
- Properties: Non-conservative, form continuous closed loops, no source charges.
- Line Integral: ∮E ⋅ dl = -dΦ/dt.
- Cylindrical Region (Radius R):
  - ⭐ Inside (r < R): E = (r/2)(dB/dt).
  - ⭐ Outside (r > R): E = (R²/2r)(dB/dt).
  - 📌 Condition: E is tangential to the circular path.
- ⚠️ Trap: Electric potential (V) does NOT exist for induced E-fields.
- ⚠️ Trap: EMF on a chord AB = (Area of triangle OAB) * (dB/dt).

9. EDDY CURRENTS
- Definition: Circular current loops induced in solid metal conductors.
- Effect: Opposes motion (Magnetic Damping) and generates heat.
- Reduction: Use laminated cores or cut slots in metal plates.
- 💡 Shortcut: If magnet falls through conducting ring, a < g; if through broken ring, a = g.

- ⚠️ Broken Ring: Magnet falling through broken ring has a = g (no current loop).
